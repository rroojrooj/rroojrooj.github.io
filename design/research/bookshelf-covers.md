# Bookshelf covers: where each one came from

_26 October 2026. The files are in `docs/bookshelf/covers/` (`<slug>.jpg` and `<slug>-large.jpg`)._

Each cover is the common English edition, picked by eye from Open Library's scans. Translations and
non-English editions, library stickers, large-print and proof copies, and very small scans were
skipped. Blitzscaling is the one exception: Open Library only has its title page, so its jacket
comes from the publisher's own cover image (images.penguinrandomhouse.com). The Intelligent
Investor's scan had a blue strip of spine on its right edge, which was trimmed. Every file was
re-encoded from raw pixels, so no EXIF, ICC, XMP or comment data is kept.

To swap a cover: find the edition on openlibrary.org, take its cover ID, and download
`https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/id/<cover ID>-L.jpg`. Then make the two files as README.md says.
